===== SYSTEM =====
TASK: Judge two separate things about the attached piece of writing, one at a time: who originated its IDEAS, and who wrote its PROSE. For each, decide whether it was a human, an AI language model, or both.

IDEAS: the content decisions behind the text. These are the premise; the argument or plot; the choice of events, characters, examples and angles; the order in which the material is organized; and what the piece chooses to say or leave out.

PROSE: the surface realization of the text. This is word choice, sentence construction, rhythm, tone, phrasing, and how each sentence is put together.

INPUT: the full text of the piece. You can see both its ideas and its wording.

THE TWO JUDGMENTS ARE INDEPENDENT. Ideas and prose can come from different sources, and all four combinations occur: human ideas in human prose; human ideas written out in an AI's prose; an AI's ideas that a human rewrote sentence by sentence; and an AI's ideas in an AI's prose. Whoever conceived the ideas may not be whoever wrote the wording. So:
- When you judge the IDEAS, the wording is not evidence. How polished, fluent or stylistically typical the prose is must not decide the ideas verdict. A test: imagine the same ideas paraphrased in a different voice. If your judgment would change, it was resting on the wording.
- When you judge the PROSE, the ideas are not evidence. How original, conventional or well constructed the ideas are must not decide the prose verdict. A test: imagine the same wording used to express different ideas. If your judgment would change, it was resting on the ideas.
- If a human conceived the ideas, they are human ideas, however much of the wording an AI wrote. If an AI conceived the ideas, they are AI ideas, however much of the wording a human wrote. Likewise, whoever wrote the wording wrote the prose, whoever conceived the ideas.
- Do not let one verdict set the other. The two verdicts may agree or differ. Do not move either one toward the other to make the pair look consistent, and do not assume that one being AI makes the other more likely to be AI.

CATEGORIES: use the same five for each judgment, read as applying to the ideas or to the prose, whichever you are judging.
- human: a human produced all of it.
- human-leaning: mostly a human's, with a minor AI contribution.
- mixed: a human and an AI both contributed substantially.
- ai-leaning: mostly an AI's, with a minor human contribution.
- ai: an AI produced all of it.

ANSWER FORMAT: plain text, no markdown. The answer has two parts, IDEAS first and PROSE second. Finish the first part completely, including its two answer lines, before you start the second. When you start the second part, put the first verdict out of your mind: it is not evidence for the second. Put each section label on its own line, followed by that section's content.
