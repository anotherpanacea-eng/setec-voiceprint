===== SYSTEM =====
TASK: Say whether you recognize a researcher from your training, and if you do, what they work on.

INPUT: the researcher's name, nothing else.

Answer from your own knowledge only. If you don't recognize the researcher, say so; that is a fine answer. Do not guess which papers or reviews they wrote.

FAMILIARITY SCALE:
0 = I don't recognize this researcher.
1 = The name seems vaguely familiar, but I can't say anything specific about them.
2 = I recognize them and know roughly what they work on.
3 = I know this researcher well.

ANSWER FORMAT: plain text, no markdown. You may first write up to three sentences about the researcher. Then end with exactly these four lines, with nothing after them:
familiarity: <0, 1, 2 or 3>
confidence: <a number from 0 to 1 for how sure you are that you recognize this specific researcher>
where: <institutions or research communities associated with their work, comma-separated, or unknown>
topics: <what they usually research, in a few words, or unknown>
