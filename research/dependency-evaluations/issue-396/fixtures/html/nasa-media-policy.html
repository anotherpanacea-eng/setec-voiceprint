NASA Images and Media Usage Guidelines

NASA content, including images, audio, video, and computer files used in the rendition of three-dimensional models, generally are not subject to copyright in the United States.

You may use this material for educational or informational purposes, including photo collections, textbooks, public exhibits, computer graphical simulations, and Internet web pages.

NASA should be acknowledged as the source of the material. If a NASA image includes an identifiable person, using the image for commercial purposes may infringe that person’s right of privacy or publicity.

Some NASA material can contain copyrights held by other people or organizations. A copyright notice identifies protected material, and permission must be obtained from the copyright owner.
