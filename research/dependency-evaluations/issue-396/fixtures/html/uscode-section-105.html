§ 105. Subject matter of copyright: United States Government works

Copyright protection under this title is not available for any work of the United States Government, but the United States Government is not precluded from receiving and holding copyrights transferred to it by assignment, bequest, or otherwise.

Historical and Revision Notes

The basic premise of this section is the same as that of section 8 of the statute now in effect: works produced for the United States Government by its officers and employees should not be subject to copyright.

The provision applies the principle to unpublished works as well as published works. It avoids placing a restriction on the Government’s receipt and holding of copyrights transferred to it.
