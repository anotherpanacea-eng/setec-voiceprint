### Changed

**`conformal_gate` — state the tie-inclusive flag rule and count calibration authors.** The `--fpr-bound` output now says that a score equal to the threshold is flagged, so on discretized scores a new point tied with the threshold can push the flag rate above q. Calibration lines may read `score,author`; when several texts share an author, `results.n_calibration_authors` reports the author count and a warning names the author as the exchangeable unit, including when too few authors exist to express the chosen `fpr_bound`. A test pins n = 30, q = 0.05 to the calibration maximum and n below 20 to abstention.
