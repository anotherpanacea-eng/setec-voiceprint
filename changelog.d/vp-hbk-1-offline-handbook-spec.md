### Added

Planning spec for an offline stylometry handbook in the Voiceprint app (fleet card VP-HBK-1), in `specs/vp-offline-handbook.md`.

- **Sources:** it inventories and reconciles the existing ones. The capability cards are authoritative for tools and the glossary for signals. The private primer is not published without the owner's ruling.
- **Entries:** one per public tool, under a nine-section template that renders metadata from the cards.
- **Tiers:** alpha, optional, future and not-offline, matched to what the app ships.
- **Packaging:** offline HTML with a local search index, capability-id anchors and a coverage check.

Nothing is written or built.
