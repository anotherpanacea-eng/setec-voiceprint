### Changed

Register `verbatim_cover._tokens` (tokenizer) and `verbatim_cover._content_fingerprint` (fingerprint) at their existing owner. The registry imports the same objects directly. Each behavior digest binds the defining function plus the `_FP_SEP`, `_tokens` and `_TOKEN` source it reads. The inventory check requires those owner bindings, and the plain `re` and `hashlib` imports, to be single, undecorated and unchanged from the merge base. It resolves registered imports through the owner and leaves same-named independent definitions and the inline `_TOKEN` uses as unresolved candidates. Characterization covers both callables, including NFC/NFD, `Straße`, the Kelvin sign, a punctuation-only variant, and the `ab`/`a b` token boundary. No consumer changed, and tokenizer and fingerprint outputs are unchanged.
