### Changed

Register `paragraph_parser.split_paragraphs` (paragraph splitter) and `paragraph_parser.split_sentences` (sentence splitter) at their existing owner. The registry imports the same objects directly. Each behavior digest binds the defining function plus the `_PARAGRAPH_SPLIT` or `_SENTENCE_END` source it reads. The inventory check requires those owner bindings, and the plain `re` import, to be single, undecorated and unchanged from the merge base. It resolves registered names read through the owner module or its flat `paragraph_parser` launcher to their rows, fails attribute stores, aliases and argument passing through that module object, and leaves same-named independent splitters as unresolved candidates. Characterization covers both callables, including blank-line, CRLF and whitespace-only separators, the ASCII-quote, lowercase-word, abbreviation and non-ASCII-capital sentence boundaries, and the paragraph-break case where `split_sentences` differs from `split_sentences_regex`. No consumer changed, and splitter outputs are unchanged.
