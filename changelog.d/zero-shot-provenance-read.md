### Added

- `zero_shot_provenance_read` (new surface `zero_shot_provenance_read`): one frontier reader's zero-shot provenance account of a single text, using the prompts of *Frontier models need only one text to unmask its human or AI author* (arXiv:2611.26216), vendored verbatim with their MIT license under `references/unmasking-authors-prompts/`. `--task ideas-prose` reports the reader's stated AI share and label for the ideas and for the prose with the evidence it cites; `--task which-model` reports the model and lab it names with its stated confidence. Every cited quote is checked against the target. Run it free by rendering the prompt (`--emit-prompt`) and passing a saved answer (`--response`), or send it to an Anthropic reader (`--reader anthropic`). `--generator-lab` refuses a reader from a lab that wrote or edited the target. No verdict and no 0.5 cut; naming a human author (the paper's RQ3) is not wired.
